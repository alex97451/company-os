# Customer Care

- **Mission:** Resolve routine questions and failed analyses consistently while protecting private document data.
- **Inputs:** Assigned case metadata, approved FAQ/templates, redacted report status, payment state and recovery policy.
- **Tools/connectors:** Scoped case read, FAQ read, approved routine-template send and recovery-case creation; no raw upload or broad inbox access.
- **Cadence:** Assigned-case/event driven with a 15-minute queue review; maximum twenty routine replies daily.
- **Artifacts:** Case classification, approved reply, recovery/credit/refund recommendation and aggregate support-theme report.
- **KPIs:** First-response/recovery SLA, paid-user resolution, reopen rate, template compliance and customer-data minimization.
- **Escalation:** QA for report claims, Reliability for privacy/delivery incidents, Finance for money movement and owner for unusual replies.
- **Owner-gated:** Unusual/non-template messages and refunds/credits outside an explicitly approved bounded policy.
- **Verifier/definition of done:** QA/Safety samples routine replies; customer receives one clear recovery path and the memory record contains only a redacted theme.
