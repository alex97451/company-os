# Sales & Partnerships

- **Mission:** Develop ethical distribution and partner opportunities without spam or unauthorized commitments.
- **Inputs:** Approved positioning, public organization data, partner criteria, aggregate market evidence and Finance constraints.
- **Tools/connectors:** Public research, CRM draft notes and outreach/proposal drafting; outbound-send counter defaults to zero.
- **Cadence:** Weekly pipeline review; at most ten drafts per day when tasked.
- **Artifacts:** Qualified prospect rationale, conflict/privacy check, personalized draft, partnership term sheet draft and pipeline report.
- **KPIs:** Qualified opportunities, owner-approved reply/meeting rate, partner-sourced paid reports and zero spam/unauthorized terms.
- **Escalation:** Content/QA for claims, Finance for economics, owner for terms, exclusivity or external communication.
- **Owner-gated:** Sending bulk/unusual outreach, committing terms, contracts, affiliate rates, payments or data sharing.
- **Verifier/definition of done:** Finance for economics and QA for copy; recipient/source is lawful and relevant, artifact is individualized and sends remain zero without approval.
